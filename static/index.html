How this works

HTDemucs 6-stem is a pretrained neural network separating vocals, bass, drums, guitar, piano, and remaining instruments on the Colab server. Browser DSP mixes stems, visualizes the FFT spectrum, applies a browser AudioWorklet granular pitch effect, tone shaping, microphone gain and delayed echo locally.

Microphone access requires HTTPS or localhost and permission from your browser. Wear headphones to prevent feedback.
